Ask a Question
How long does sea freight from China to Ghana take?

Transit time depends on the port of loading and the shipping line, but most sailings from China to Tema take roughly 35 to 50 days. Air freight usually takes 5 to 10 days. We will confirm the timeline when we quote.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL (Full Container Load) means you book a whole container for your goods. LCL (Less than Container Load) means your goods share a container with other shippers, which is cheaper for smaller volumes.

Do you handle customs clearance only?

Yes. Even if you booked shipping elsewhere, we can clear your cargo at Tema, Takoradi or Kotoka International Airport and deliver it to you.

What documents do I need to import?

Typically a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, and any permits your goods need. We will tell you exactly what is required for your cargo.

Can you insure my goods?

Yes. We can arrange cargo insurance so your shipment is covered against loss or damage in transit. Ask for it when you request your quote.
