OUR SERVICES

Excellent Majj Plus Ltd

At Excellent Majj Plus Ltd, we provide reliable freight forwarding and logistics solutions to individuals, businesses, importers, and exporters. Our aim is to make the transportation of goods easier, safer, and more efficient through professional service and dependable logistics support.

01

Air Freight

We provide reliable air freight solutions for the transportation of goods to local and international destinations. Our service is suitable for customers who require timely cargo delivery, efficient shipment handling, and dependable international shipping arrangements.

02

Sea Freight

We offer sea freight services for importers and exporters, supporting the movement of different types and volumes of cargo across international destinations. We aim to provide practical and cost-effective shipping solutions for businesses engaged in international trade.

03

Inland Transportation

We provide dependable road transportation services for the movement of goods from ports, warehouses, and collection points to their final destinations. We also facilitate cross-border cargo transportation through these Ghana border locations:

Paga Border Hamile Border Kulungugu Border

Our goal is to ensure smooth cargo movement, reliable transportation, and efficient delivery across these routes.

04

Clearing & Forwarding

We assist customers with customs clearance and forwarding procedures for goods entering or leaving Ghana. We support shipment documentation and clearance processes to help make international cargo movement easier and more convenient.

05

Warehousing

We provide warehousing services for the safe storage and handling of goods. Our service supports customers who require temporary storage of cargo before transportation, distribution, or final delivery.

ABOUT US

A Ghanaian company built on trust

Our Motto "One Good Turn Deserves Another." This reflects our belief in mutual respect, dependable partnerships, and treating every customer with care and fairness.

Excellent Majj Plus Ltd is a Ghanaian-owned company based in Ghana, committed to providing dependable services across freight forwarding, clearing and forwarding, warehousing, transportation, and other business sectors.

Our goal is to make business operations and the movement of goods easier through reliable service, professionalism, and a commitment to customer satisfaction. We provide air, sea, and inland transportation solutions, helping customers move goods efficiently while supporting their logistics and supply chain needs.

Beyond freight and logistics, our business activities extend to:

Rentals Hospitality Building and Construction Quarry and Mining Oil and Gas Borehole Drilling Food Industry Sanitation Services

At Excellent Majj Plus Ltd, we believe that strong business relationships are built on trust, integrity, teamwork, and service excellence. We strive to understand our customers' needs and deliver practical solutions that create lasting value.

Whether serving individuals, businesses, or international partners, we are dedicated to building relationships that stand the test of time.
